## Background

I am an embedded software engineer with experience designing and implementing firmware for microcontrollers, real-time operating systems, and safety-critical systems. My work spans from bare-metal C on Cortex-M targets to AUTOSAR-based automotive ECUs.

## Research Interests

- **Real-time scheduling** — priority-based and EDF schedulers, response-time analysis
- **Formal verification** — model checking and static analysis for safety-critical firmware
- **Hardware-software co-design** — FPGA prototyping, custom peripherals, DMA pipelines
- **Communication protocols** — CAN, SPI, I²C, UART, Ethernet (lwIP), custom on-wire formats
